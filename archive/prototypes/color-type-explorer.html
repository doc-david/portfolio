Brewed With Real Labuyo Peppers

Whether its to wind down after a long day or to spice up a great party, Doctor's Brew is the perfect drink for any occasion. A family recipe passed down generations throughout the Philippines, this specialty brew is carefully crafted using real Saling Labuyo peppers, preserving the unique sweet and sour taste while toning down the spice.

This was a package design project of a fictional beer company that I came up with. Using inspiration from hot sauces packaging, old time shipping companies, and the culture of the Philippines I created bottle and case designs.

An All-In-One
Student Life App

Schedules, grades, events, clubs, there's plenty to keep track of during your college career. Buff Life makes it just a little bit easier for you to quickly check any information you need at a moments notice while promoting the various activities around campus to wind down.

This project was inspired by the many issues with CU Boulder's mobile version of its current student portal. Not having an app and remaining on the mobile browser, the UI remained largely unchanged. There were many times I and many others had to frantically login and search through the website to find the info I needed before a class or exam. I created this prototype to make the UI cleaner and stylistic while giving students the info they need fast.

A Fast Casual Business Analysis

How does a fast-casual restaurant like Cava design the flow of information to keep things efficient for both the consumer and the employee? Between the assembly line, the menus, and the signs around the store, everything is set up so you can go from the door to your bowl without anyone having to explain a thing.

This was a research project for my Information Ecosystems class, where I looked at a fast casual restaurant as an information system. I visited during the dinner rush and again in the afternoon, took pictures, sketched a map of the store, and ordered my own bowl to see the whole process from the customer's side. Using distributed cognition, I broke down how the space, the staff, and the timing all work together to keep the line moving.

    It's... A Little Too Effective?

    More health, more damage, more powerful abilities. Every new expansion of the Pokémon Trading Card Game gives you a reason to buy in, but it also pushes older cards further out of the game. It's called power creep, a common game design trap, and it got so bad that official tournaments only allow cards from the newest sets.

    This was a final project for my Programming for Information Science class, where I used Python, Pandas, and Seaborn to measure just how strong the cards have gotten. I combined a CSV of every card from 1999 to 2023 with an API for the 2024 releases, then charted the highest HP card of each year by evolution stage. The strongest Basic card of 2024 has more than double the HP of the strongest Stage 2 card from 1999.
